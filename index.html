Live client brief — MSc Digital Marketing, LJMU

£2,000. 326,510 impressions.
Let's talk about what happened.

The brief

Maggie's Wirral needed a 12-month, £40,000 campaign strategy — plus proof it would actually work. So the brief included a real £2,000 paid media pilot to test the thinking before anyone committed the full budget.

The thinking

I segmented the audience three ways — demographic (25–65), psychographic (VALS), behavioural — and built a SOSTAC-structured plan against SMART objectives: 60% traffic uplift, 5% Meta CTR, CPC under £1.50, 30% donation increase.

The idea

Build tightly themed ad groups around informational search intent rather than generic charity terms, and run a SEMrush keyword gap analysis against Macmillan Cancer Support and cancersupportuk.org to find the openings they'd missed.

My role

Digital Marketing Campaign Strategist. I planned, built and ran the pilot, audited the site, and put together the KPI framework the full campaign would report against.

What I learned

A tiny budget forces honesty. £2,000 doesn't leave room for a campaign that just looks good on a slide — every ad group had to earn its spend, which is a better lesson than any case study with a bigger number attached.

What I'm actually checking

Not a generic audit template — this is the sequence I actually work through, built from what's caught real problems on real sites.

Full Screaming Frog crawl first — crawl errors, broken redirect chains, orphaned pages. This is where the biggest wins usually hide, and where a 29/100 mobile PageSpeed score gets found before anyone else notices.

Ahrefs and SEMrush gap analysis against real competitors, not vanity keywords — mapping what's found to commercial versus informational intent so content gets built for what people actually want when they land.

Rewriting title tags and meta descriptions using actual Search Console impression and CTR data — not guesswork. This alone lifted CTR by 2–5% across tracked landing pages at Ethinos.

Restructuring how pages link to each other so authority flows to the pages that actually need to rank, instead of getting stuck on the homepage.

Article, FAQ and BreadcrumbList markup, implemented properly — contributed to a 55% increase in rich-result appearances on client sites.

Identifying genuine high-authority link opportunities via Ahrefs and coordinating outreach — slow work, but it's the difference that actually moves domain rating.

Monthly reporting on Core Web Vitals, ranking movement and traffic trends via GA4 and Search Console — built so a client can actually prioritise the next investment, not just admire a graph.
